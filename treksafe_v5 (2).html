Rescue Workflow

From deterioration to
dispatch in seconds.

01
Continuous Sensing
The band watches — even offline.
Heart rate, blood oxygen (SpO₂), and six-axis motion are sampled every second. All processing runs locally on the microcontroller. No phone, no cellular signal required. Data is stored if connectivity drops and synced when restored.
Every 1 second · Fully offline capable
02
Smart Triage
Not every high heart rate is an emergency.
Multi-sensor fusion cross-references vitals against motion data. A high HR from steep climbing is normal. High HR plus falling SpO₂ plus sustained no-movement — that is an emergency. This logic eliminates false positives and prevents alert fatigue.
Sensor fusion · Zero false-positive design
03
15-Second Window
The pilgrim gets one chance to confirm.
When the threshold logic fires, the band triggers a loud buzzer and shows a display prompt. The pilgrim has 15 seconds to press the button and confirm they are safe. One press cancels everything. This window respects autonomy while preserving response speed.
15 seconds · Conscious confirmation only
04
Automatic Dispatch
No response triggers rescue — automatically.
If the 15-second window expires with no interaction, the device transmits a LoRa packet with live GPS coordinates to the nearest help centre gateway. The dashboard fires an alert. Staff dispatch with one click. Family receives a push notification. No phone call needed.
Auto-dispatch · GPS coordinates included

Technology

Built for where
nothing else works.

Every component chosen for the specific constraints of high-altitude mountain terrain.

MAX30100
Optical Pulse Oximeter
Measures heart rate and blood oxygen simultaneously using infrared. Reads every second, no discomfort.
SpO₂ ±2% accuracy · HR ±3 BPM
MPU6050
6-Axis Motion Sensor
3-axis accelerometer and 3-axis gyroscope detect falls, sustained stillness, and movement patterns.
±2g range · Configurable fall threshold
NEO-6M GPS
Location Tracking
Sends precise coordinates with every emergency alert. Responders know exactly where to go.
2.5m accuracy · Cold start 27s
RYLR998 LoRa
Long-Range Radio
915MHz sub-GHz frequency penetrates rock and mountain terrain. 5–7km range, no cellular required.
915MHz · 5–7km range · Ultra-low power
NodeMCU ESP8266
Microcontroller + WiFi
Runs all sensor fusion and alert logic locally on-device. Built-in WiFi for gateway relay at help centres.
80MHz · Built-in WiFi · 3.3V logic
SSD1306 OLED
Wrist Display
Shows HR, SpO₂, GPS status, and alert prompts. High contrast, readable in direct mountain sunlight.
128×64px · I2C · 0.96 inch
